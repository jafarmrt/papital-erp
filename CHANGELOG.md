An email is masked in every audit row, and the ERP's buyer list shows it. Nothing changes while the site's email is off (8e's settings); the site's pages follow in the next part.

Only an order whose phone was proven when it was placed opens this way (migration 0025, `phone_proven`): orders placed before it, while the texts were off, or with an unproven account phone open only with their own tracking key

- The new site on a phone, the checkout's sum and the wholesale request (v12 phase 8a, site review 2.3, 2.4 and 2.12): a bar along the bottom of a phone with خانه، دسته‌ها، جست‌وجو، سبد and حساب من, with its own «دسته‌ها» and «جست‌وجو» pages; a summary right above «ثبت سفارش» with each item's amount, the shipping of the method picked, the discount and, large, «مبلغ قابل پرداخت», reckoned with the same rule the ERP prices the order by (`payableWith`), and an order placed only at the amount the summary showed (Storefront API: the shipping quote also answers `lines`, each line at the price this buyer pays; security-reviewed); a «درخواست خرید عمده» page at /wholesale (a signed-in buyer writes only the business; a guest proves the phone by SMS first), linked from the home page, «حساب من» and the footer; and a robot trap on the corporate gift, wholesale and sign-up forms (a hidden field and a signed time the form was made), checked on the site's own server so a trapped form never reaches the ERP; the phone proof cookies are on the whole site, so the pages' own data requests carry them

The server's address is checked like any outgoing address (never an inside one), a user name and password never go without encryption, a stored password never follows a changed server, the user name and sender are masked in the audit log, and a failure is told in plain Persian. `nodemailer` joins the server's packages (approved in the start plan)
- One visitor slowing the site for everyone (v12 phase 7, site review 1.4): the site app allows each visitor 30 searches a minute (more is a 429 page for that visitor only), a search the app cannot answer from its cache names the visitor to the ERP (`X-Storefront-Client-IP`), and the ERP gives each named visitor 120 catalog reads a minute of their own (429) on top of the shared limit. The ERP keeps the list of items on the site 5 seconds for the public reads (catalog, search, facets, campaign cards, same-design cards) and reads many at once once; publishing, unpublishing and saving the site settings forget it at once, while an order, a quote, a group's prices, the feed and a product's own page still read the database.
- Fake site orders that hold stock (v12 phase 7, site review 1.3 and 2.10): while the site signs in by SMS, a guest's order now needs a phone proof for its own phone (`phoneProof` from `POST /auth/otp/verify`, used up inside the order's transaction): without one, or with another phone's or a used one, it is 422 `STOREFRONT_PHONE_PROOF_REQUIRED` and leaves no order, customer, reservation or text behind; an order refused for another reason (an item short) rolls the proof back for the next try, a repeat of the same request still replays its order, and a signed-in account needs none. The checkout asks for the code on the same form at the first «ثبت سفارش» and keeps the proof in an httpOnly cookie of that page only. A retail buyer (no live buyer group) orders at most five of an item, at the quote and at the order (422 `STOREFRONT_RETAIL_LINE_CAP`), and the cart stops «یکی بیشتر» at that cap or at the count on the site. With the texts off nothing changes for a guest. The customer is still made when the order is placed; moving it to the payment went to phase 8 (TD-1394). The branch's merge of master also set the debt registry's active count back to the number of rows.
- Site orders and their proformas (v12 phase 7, site review 1.5, 1.6 and 3.2a): the proforma of an open site order (waiting for payment, a receipt under review or an on-account approval) can no longer be edited, finalized or voided through the ERP's own document paths (409 `DOCUMENT_SITE_ORDER_OPEN` naming the site order; finalizing it used to leave the order «waiting for payment» on the site, so the buyer could pay twice, and editing it put the invoice out of step with the amount the payment confirmation posts); the site's own payment confirmation, on-account approval and cancel move the order first in the same transaction, so they pass. «ثبت سفارش» off now closes only new orders: while the site is on a buyer still reads an order and sends its receipt (with the tracking key or from «حساب من») and the reservation sweep keeps running (it used to stop, keeping the stock reserved, then cancel every overdue order at once). An order whose document was made a draft in the ERP before this change is cancelled and its draft voided; one whose document became a final invoice is no longer picked by the sweep, so such orders cannot fill its batch of 50 and stop every other order from expiring (voiding that invoice in the ERP, still allowed, lets the sweep cancel the order). A treasury receipt linked to such a proforma from the ERP is not refused yet (TD-1393).
- The new site's header on a phone (v12 phase 7, site review 1.1 and 1.2): on a 360 or 390 px screen the header was about 400 px wide, so the page slid sideways and the menu button was half off the screen; below the `sm` width the search is now an icon that opens a full-width search row and the logo and gaps are smaller, so menu, logo, search and cart fit (checked in a browser at 360 px: no sideways scroll). The phone menu and the search row close on every page change; the menu used to stay open over the new page after a link was tapped.
- Featured images on the new site (v12 phase 7, start plan docs/roadmap/V12_SITE_NEXT_START_PLAN.md §4): since v10.1.71 «تصویر شاخص محصول شود» set the item's picture but not the library cover mark the site's product cards, its admin list and the WooCommerce featured-image push read, so every product showed without a featured image. The button now also makes that file the item's one cover in the library (the item's other marks taken off, each change with a «کتابخانه تصاویر» audit row in the same transaction), and a site card without a cover shows the item's first library photo. `npm run media:cover-repair` (dry run; `-- --apply` to write) marks the cover of items whose picture came from the library before this change, using the item's audit trail, lists products whose picture is not in the library (the site cannot show those until a photo is uploaded there) and says how many of the marked products are linked to WooCommerce (while «ارسال تصویر شاخص» is on, the next pushes replace their shop featured image). A picture changed from the item form or an Excel import does not move the cover mark yet (TD-1392). The flaky item-code regression (`itemCodeChangeTests.ts`, red on master 4 Nov 2026 when two random codes were equal) never hands out the same code twice.
