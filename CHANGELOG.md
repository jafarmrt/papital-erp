### v11.6.0 — پایان فاز ۷: آزمون کامل و رفع‌های پایانی نسخهٔ ۱۱
- One word for colleagues (owner request): screens and messages say «همکار» / «همکاران» instead of «کارکن» / «کارکنان» and «کارگر» (workshop review, messenger bot settings and messages, dead-letter filter, schedule picker, data export notes). The insurance term «بیمه سهم کارگر» stays as on the official insurance form (owner choice); «بانک رفاه کارگران» is a bank name.
- Closed requisition rows (v11 phase 7.2, TD-1391 closed): one rule in `src/lib/procurement/requisitionRows.ts` for a closed requisition row and for spreading an ordered quantity over the rows of one item, used by «تبدیل به سفارش», the rebuild after a void or an order edit and the rebuild after a draft order is voided (a void runs both). A closed row (flag, closure note or the legacy shape: «ordered» with less than requested) keeps its status and the quantity it had ordered, at most what its own linked live orders still hold, and gets the flag; the rest of the item's quantity is spread over the open rows only, the last open row taking any excess; a closed row receives no more than it had ordered and a receipt's leftover goes on the last open row; and the over-order check counts a closed row as needing only what it had ordered (in an order edit, as stored). When the procurement rebuild runs in a void, the documents step only unlinks the voided order, so the two never read different order sets. Before, the draft-order void reopened a legacy closed row as «pending», and the spread let an open row absorb a closed row's quantity, so the open row showed less left to order than it needed. Rows already shifted before this change are not repaired. Vitest `requisitionRowClosureTd1391.test.ts`, `salesCrmV11.test.ts`.
- Findings of the v11 full test (v11 phase 7.2, F1 to F6, card ت۶۲): a receipt or payment is linked directly to one document, when recorded or by «انتقال به سند دیگر», only when that document is issued (final or proforma), as a part already was (422 `SETTLEMENT_DOCUMENT_NOT_ISSUED`); what a receipt, payment or cheque dated in a closed fiscal year settles no longer changes: moving its parts, moving it to another document or on account, taking a cheque's parts off and deleting a cheque that settles documents are refused (409 `SETTLEMENT_IN_CLOSED_YEAR`), while a receipt of an open year may still settle an old invoice; a new item from the Excel import starts the «item» workflow as the item form does, and with an active definition its opening voucher waits for the approval; a document void stores the real instant in `documents.deleted_at` (it stored the business wall clock 3.5 hours ahead) and migration 0007 moves the old void times it can prove from their activity row, recorded in `legacy_date_repairs` (kind `timezone`); a rejected item definition takes its opening stock back with reversal Kardex rows (`reversal_of_id`) and locks the item FOR NO KEY UPDATE; and a direct link, the move to another document and its list of documents ask the documents section, as parts do. Regression `closingFindingsV11Tests.ts` (four cases), `reg_item_rejected_opening_withdrawn_v11_6_1j_s13` updated.
