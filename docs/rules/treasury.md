# Treasury and Cheques

Receipts, payments, transfers, cheques, petty cash and bank accounts: every treasury row goes through one path.

Core rules (one line each, with their guards) are in `AGENTS.md`. This file holds the full rules of the area; the history of each rule (versions, debt rows, decisions) is in `docs/rules/archive/AGENTS_V10.md` at commit `c3c9858` (archives removed in v11.1.0), and a «§N» below refers to that file's numbering.

- **Cheque ↔ Voucher Link:** Every voucher of a cheque's lifecycle (registration, in collection, cleared, bounced, spent) carries `journal_vouchers.source_cheque_id` (migration 0047); find a cheque's vouchers by it, never by the cheque number (not unique). `deleteCheque` refuses while an unlinked legacy voucher of a number shared by another cheque remains. A cheque with a permanent voucher that has no reversal is never deleted (409 naming the voucher), and the cheque row menu offers no status change or delete in a terminal status (`chequeHasNextStep` in `src/lib/treasury/chequeTransitions.ts`, the one transition table of the server and the browser). A bounced paid cheque posts Dr cheque payables / Cr the supplier; returning a bounced received cheque to its drawer posts Dr the customer / Cr the protested-cheques account. Cheques are rial only: `createCheque` refuses any other currency; foreign receipts and payments go through the treasury form at an exchange rate. A cleared (`passed`) cheque counts in its bank account's treasury balance — received adds, paid subtracts — and always keeps the bank it cleared through; clearing into a bank account without a ledger account is refused, and a cheque step whose voucher needs an account the mapping cannot resolve (clearing, collection, bounce of a received cheque, spending) is refused with 422 `CHEQUE_ACCOUNT_MAPPING_MISSING` before anything changes (`requireChequeAccounts` in `chequeLifecycle.service.ts`). The treasury and invoice-settlement forms have no cheque method: `createTreasuryTransaction` refuses `method: 'cheque'` and cheques are registered only from the cheque book; legacy cheque-method transactions stay voidable and never count in a bank's treasury balance. A receipt or payment of party type «other», and a personnel one with purpose «other», posts to a counter account the user chooses from `GET /accounting/treasury/contra-accounts` (an active subsidiary account other than the control accounts of accounting.md, among them the insurance, payroll tax, eidi and other deductions payables, the employee advances and every inventory account of group 14 since v11 phase 6.1b (M6), and the two year-closing accounts; `requireChoosableContraAccount` in `src/services/accounting/treasury/partyContraAccount.ts`, else 422 `TREASURY_CONTRA_ACCOUNT_INVALID`, for a control account naming the book its entries come from; the statutory remittance penalty keeps its own 422 for 3204 and 3206; guard `reg_treasury_contra_control_v11_6_1b_m6`); a personnel receipt or payment requires its purpose, and both are stored on the row (`treasury_transactions.purpose` / `contra_account_id`, migration 0060). A treasury receipt or payment is linked only to what exists: its party id must be in the table of its party type (customer / supplier in `customers` with that type or «both», personnel in `personnel`, «other» has none) and its document, locked `FOR SHARE` under the bank lock, must be active, a sales invoice or proforma for a receipt and a purchase document for a payment, with a customer / supplier party of the document — the document's `party_id` when the row carries a party id, else the party name equals the document's trimmed buyer name (`resolveTreasuryPartyName` / `assertTreasuryDocumentLink` in `src/services/accounting/treasury/treasuryLinks.ts`, else 422); a personnel receipt, payment or cheque needs the personnel id (422 `TREASURY_PERSONNEL_REQUIRED`, `assertPersonnelPartyId`, same file). A receipt or payment linked to a document and sent without a party id takes, in its own transaction before the link check and the voucher, the document's party id and the party's current name when the row is on the document's side, the name sent is the document's buyer name or the party's current name, and the party is live and of that type or «both» (`documentPartyOfTreasuryLink`, same file; the invoice settlement form also sends the document's `partyId`), so the treasury row and its voucher row stay on the party's account card and delete guard after a rename; another name is still 422 `TREASURY_DOCUMENT_PARTY_MISMATCH` and a legacy document without a party keeps the exact-name rule. The linked document's currency (empty = IRR) is the row's currency on create as on relink, one rule in `assertTreasuryDocumentLink` (422 `TREASURY_DOCUMENT_CURRENCY_MISMATCH` before any write). A cheque's party row follows its party type with the treasury form's rule (customer → trade receivables, supplier → trade payables, personnel → wages payable or employee advance by its required purpose, «other» and personnel «other» → the chosen counter account, detail of the party's own type); the account is stored on the cheque (`cheques.party_account_id` / `purpose`, migration 0061) and its registration, paid-cheque bounce and return post to it (`chequePartyPosting` in `src/services/accounting/treasury/chequePartyAccount.ts`); a cheque from before v9.0.84 keeps the old rule and, when its party type differs, is listed by the financial health check (`cheque_party_account_legacy`), never rewritten. Spending a received cheque needs the supplier's id (`transfereePartyId`, a supplier or «both» party; else 422 `CHEQUE_TRANSFEREE_REQUIRED` / `TREASURY_PARTY_INVALID`): its voucher debits trade payables with that supplier's detail and the payee name is read from the supplier row; the status form picks the supplier from the list, never a typed name. A treasury receipt or payment, a bank transfer or a cheque recorded without a voucher (`createVoucher: false`, e.g. opening balances) needs the permission `accounting.treasury_no_voucher` (granted to no role by default), computed in the route with `userHasRoleOrPermission` and passed as `allowNoVoucher` (never read from the body), else 403; the forms show the option only to its holders and the financial health check lists such entries (`treasury_without_voucher`, `src/services/accounting/treasury/noVoucherTreasury.ts`). A bank-to-bank transfer is voided as a whole: `voidTreasuryTransaction` finds the other side (same shared voucher, opposite type, `bank_transfer`, `transferSideIds`), locks both banks then both treasury rows, and reverses both rows and both balances and voids the shared voucher once; the remaining side of a transfer half-voided before v8.0.73 is voided without touching the voucher. A reversal row (the void of another treasury row) is never voided (409): to record it again, enter a new transaction; legacy revived rows without a voucher (even reversal depth ≥ 2) are listed by the health check `treasury_without_voucher`, and the business invariants I15 / I16 (`src/tests/invariants/bankInvariants.ts`) check each bank against its ledger rows of every voucher status. A document's paid amount counts its completed treasury rows plus each voided row whose reversal row is on the same document (`settledAmount` in `src/services/documents/documentSettlement.ts`), so a void nets to zero and a later receipt counts in full. Its remaining amount and settlement status also subtract its final, non-voided sales returns (net of lines + VAT + service charge, `returnedAmount`, `fetchReturnCredits` in `src/services/documents/documentReturnCredit.ts`), as the return voucher does on the customer's account. A bank's current balance is its treasury balance — opening balance + treasury transactions + cleared cheques: «همگام‌سازی مانده بانک‌ها» (`recalculateAndSyncBankBalances`) locks every bank in id order and writes that balance; the general ledger difference is only reported (an opening voucher counts only when approved or permanent; a row tagged with a bank detail belongs only to that bank), and an account whose ledger with its draft vouchers equals its treasury balance is shown «در انتظار تأیید سند» (`pending_approval`, `src/lib/treasury/bankSyncStatus.ts`), never as a discrepancy. Bank account maintenance runs under the bank row lock: `updateBankAccount` locks the row `FOR UPDATE` and computes the opening-balance difference under it; treasury account codes come from `assignTreasuryAccountCode` (`src/services/accounting/treasury/bankAccountCode.ts`: an atomic counter per prefix in `document_ref_counters`, a custom code unique among active accounts, case-insensitive, `TREASURY_ACCOUNT_CODE_TAKEN`), never max + 1; `deleteBankAccount` locks the bank and is refused while it has treasury transactions or non-deleted cheques. In the same transaction it voids the account's standing opening and opening-adjustment vouchers through `voidSourceVoucher` (draft soft-deleted, approved reversed) and is refused with 409 naming the voucher when one is permanent (`voidBankOpeningVouchers` in `src/services/accounting/treasury/bankOpeningVoucher.ts`). While the account's `bank_account` approval workflow is `IN_PROGRESS`, a change of its opening balance is refused with 409 (`assertNoPendingOpeningApproval`, same file), so the opening voucher is issued only by the approval. The cheque reconciliation report compares 3101 by its credit balance and expects only paid cheques that are not cleared, bounced or returned.

- **Cheque and Treasury Order of Dates and Amounts (v11 phase 6.1a, M12, M16):** a paid cheque (one the business writes) is due on or after its issue day (422 `CHEQUE_DUE_BEFORE_ISSUE`); a received cheque's issue date is the day it is recorded, so a customer's cheque that is already due is recorded as it is. Each step is dated on or after the cheque's issue day and its earlier steps (422 `CHEQUE_STEP_BEFORE_PREVIOUS`, `src/services/accounting/treasury/chequeDateOrder.ts`; legacy non-ISO history days are skipped, and so is an issue day after today that a cheque recorded before v9.0.98 can hold). A treasury receipt, payment, transfer or cheque amount that a JS number cannot hold exactly is 422 `AMOUNT_PRECISION_EXCEEDED` (`exactTreasuryAmount`), never stored changed. Guard: `reg_cheque_and_payslip_date_order_v11_6_1a_m12`, `reg_number_bounds_v11_6_1a_m16`.

- **Petty Cash Is a Fluctuating Fund:** A petty cash fund is a treasury account of type `petty_cash`, rial only (create and edit refuse another currency with 422 `PETTY_CASH_RIAL_ONLY`, `assertPettyCashCurrency` in `src/services/accounting/treasury/bankAccountCurrency.ts`, rule `pettyCashCurrencyRefusal` in `src/lib/treasury/pettyCash.ts`, shared with the form), whose ledger account defaults to 1006 «تنخواه‌گردان». Any amount is charged to it and returned from it with an ordinary bank transfer (the fund card's «شارژ تنخواه» / «برگشت وجه» open the transfer form with the fund as destination or source), so its balance varies. Its expenses are recorded only as a statement (`POST /accounting/treasury/petty-cash/:id/expenses`, `accounting.treasury`, idempotent; `createPettyCashExpense` in `src/services/accounting/treasury/pettyCashExpense.service.ts`): one treasury payment from the fund under the fund row lock, never above its balance, which earlier statements with a draft voucher have already lowered (422 `PETTY_CASH_INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE`), with lines stored in `petty_cash_expense_lines` (migration 0116): each line needs an account and a positive amount of at most four decimals (else 422 `PETTY_CASH_LINES_INVALID`), while its description, seller and invoice number are optional and no VAT row is made (purchase VAT is not recorded, ت۱۳); a line's account is an active posting expense or cost-of-sales account the counter-account list allows (`GET /accounting/treasury/petty-cash/expense-accounts`, else 422 `PETTY_CASH_EXPENSE_ACCOUNT_INVALID`). Its draft treasury voucher debits each line's account without a detail and credits the fund's ledger account with the fund's bank-account detail (so I15 holds), written with the treasury row, the audit row, the attachments and the outbox event in one transaction; the statement is voided with the treasury void like any payment, and `GET /accounting/treasury/:id/petty-cash-lines` reads its lines. No new permission.

- **Bank Account Start Date:** A treasury account has a start date (`bank_accounts.opening_date`, migration 0134, «تاریخ آغاز حساب»): create resolves it with `resolveTreasuryWriteDate` (empty is the business today, Jalali accepted, a future day 422 `TREASURY_DATE_IN_FUTURE`), and the opening voucher of the initial balance (`issueTreasuryOpeningVoucher`, `treasury_opening`) carries it, also when a workflow issues the voucher after approval (the closed-year check then runs at create); an account saved before 0134 has none and its opening voucher takes the business today. The opening voucher and the adjustments of the opening balance are sourced vouchers (`treasury_opening`, v11 phase 3.1 M2): the voucher page only approves and finalizes them, and the balance changes in this account's form. An edit changes the date freely while no opening voucher exists, moves a draft opening voucher with it (`moveOpeningVoucherDate` in `bankOpeningVoucher.ts`, the closed-year check included), and is refused once the opening voucher is approved or permanent (422 `BANK_OPENING_DATE_LOCKED`; the account lists `openingDateLocked`, so the form's date field is read only then and stays editable over a draft), also while the opening approval is pending (the approval takes the date it finds and reads the account row FOR UPDATE in `issueTreasuryOpeningVoucher`, so an edit of the date waits for it and then meets the voucher; the workflow action's `lockEntity` takes that row before the instance row, the order of the account's edit and delete; the opening balance stays locked then, 409); an initial-balance adjustment voucher of an edit is dated today. No treasury write is dated in a closed fiscal year, with or without a voucher: `postTreasuryRow` (receipt, payment, payroll payment, petty cash expense, statutory remittance), the transfer, the cheque create and every cheque step check `FiscalPeriodService.assertOpen` (v11 M5); the reversal of a treasury row is dated today. A start date in a closed fiscal year is refused with 422 `BANK_OPENING_DATE_YEAR_CLOSED`, a message naming the date and the way out (`assertOpeningYearOpen`), at create, at the approval that issues the voucher and at the edit that gives a zero-balance account its first balance; moving the date in an edit lets each go on. Treasury currency codes compare without regard to case or spaces: `sameTreasuryCurrency` (`src/lib/treasury/treasuryCurrency.ts`, empty is IRR) in the transfer, the treasury preview, cheque clearing and the monthly insurance or tax payment, and `assertBankCurrency` for a posting (an account without a currency takes any).

- **One Treasury Path:** A treasury receipt or payment and its void are written only through `postTreasuryRow` / `reverseTreasuryRow` (`src/services/accounting/treasury/treasuryPosting.ts`): the bank balance (never negative, 422 `TREASURY_BALANCE_INSUFFICIENT` unless the caller names its own refusal), the number from `treasury_tx_number_seq`, the voucher (a callback of the caller, in the same `tx`), the row and its `TreasuryTransactionApproved` outbox event; the reversal copies the original's links (document, cheque, payslip, counter account) and moves the balance back unless the original never moved it (a treasury-form cheque-method row; a payslip cheque-method payment did, `movedBankBalance`). The treasury form, its void, the payslip payment and its void, the petty cash statement and the monthly insurance or tax payment use them; the caller takes the bank lock first through `lockTreasuryBank`, `lockTreasuryBankRow` or `lockTreasuryBanks` (TD-1384: the writers take only a `LockedTreasuryBank`, a branded type that only these helpers of `treasuryPosting.ts` make, so a write without the lock does not compile; Vitest `lockedTreasuryBank.test.ts`) and checks its own domain rules before. Only the bank-to-bank transfer pair writes its two rows itself; no file outside `src/services/accounting/treasury/` writes `treasury_transactions` or `bank_accounts` (Vitest `treasuryOnePathObsR252.test.ts`). The `TreasuryTransactionApproved` event of a treasury receipt, payment, transfer, petty cash statement or monthly insurance or tax payment, and of each void, carries no amount, description or reason (`treasuryEventPayload` / `TREASURY_EVENT_PRIVATE_KEYS` in `treasuryPosting.ts`), in the outbox and to rules and webhooks alike; instead it carries `isHighValue`, the row's rial value (amount × its own rate when foreign; none is false) at or above the setting «آستانه تراکنش کلان خزانه» (`treasury_high_value_threshold`, a whole rial amount above zero, default 500,000,000, `src/lib/settings/settingValues.ts`, read with the write's `tx` by `src/services/accounting/treasury/treasuryHighValue.ts`; the default is never written while no value is stored, and a change writes its own activity row and notifies every other system admin under the category `settings_change`, `recordTreasuryThresholdChange` in `src/services/settings/treasuryThresholdChange.ts`), except on a payslip payment and its void. The default rule «ثبت ممیزی تراکنش‌های کلان خزانه‌داری» compares `payload.isHighValue eq true`, and an installed copy still holding exactly its seeded amount condition is moved to it once at boot with an audit row (`repairSeededHighValueTreasuryRule` in `src/services/events/highValueTreasuryRuleRepair.ts`, the one exception to TD-715); a numeric comparison (`gt` … `lte`) on a field the event does not carry never matches (`RuleEngineService.evaluateSingleRule`; a null value is still 0, and «not equal» / «not in» still match a missing field), the boot logs each saved rule of the treasury event (or of every event) whose numeric comparison reads the amount, description or reason, one line per rule asking for its review (`logRulesOnMissingNumericFields` in `src/services/events/ruleMissingFieldLog.ts`), and the financial health check lists every other stored rule of the treasury event that reads such a field by kind, with the engine's own missing-field outcome (`missingFieldOutcome` in `src/lib/events/ruleMissingField.ts`, inverted under a «NOT» group, `event.payload.` read as `payload.`, node shapes, group logic and children read with the engine's own `isSingleRuleNode` / `isRuleGroupNode` / `ruleGroupParts`): a condition that no longer matches, one that now matches every treasury event (`neq`, `not_in`, an absence check, an empty «contains» or pattern; score impact) and action text printed empty (`event_rule_treasury_private_fields`, `src/services/accounting/treasuryRuleFieldHealth.ts`); a seeded rule whose action settings the owner rewrote to print the amount (its text or title) is not moved.
- **Receipts and Cheques on Invoices (v11 phase 6.1t, M11 / U2, decision ت۵۲-۵):** A customer receipt or supplier payment of the treasury form (completed, not a reversal or payslip: `canAllocateTreasuryRow`), or a cheque received from a customer or paid to a supplier that has not bounced or come back (`canAllocateCheque`, `src/lib/treasury/settlementAllocation.ts`), settles one or more documents of its party in parts (`settlement_allocations`, migration 0003: one source, treasury row or cheque, per part, amount above zero, one live part per source and document). The forms (`SettlementAllocationField`) default to the oldest open documents first (`fillOldestFirst`, by day then id, from `GET /documents/settlement-options`, read by `accounting.treasury` or `accounting.cheques` within the reader's document scope, as `GET /documents`), let the user pick documents and amounts or keep all on account, and hold the form back while the list loads or fails; a recorded source is changed from «نشاندن روی فاکتورها» (`PUT /accounting/treasury/:id/allocations` or `/accounting/cheques/:id/allocations`), which replaces its whole set (an empty set puts it all on account) and moves a direct link (`documentId`) into parts in the same transaction. Each part follows the direct-link rule (`assertTreasuryDocumentLink`: a receipt settles sales invoices and proformas, a payment purchase documents, same party and currency) and is at most what the document has left (`assertWithinDocumentRemaining` with `exact`: payable less settlements, parts and final returns, compared at the four decimals a part is stored with and without the 0.01 slack a direct link keeps, the document locked first, in id order); all parts together are at most the source's amount, and a create never takes both `documentId` and parts (422 `SETTLEMENT_ALLOCATION_WITH_DOCUMENT`). Lock order: a treasury source locks its documents (60) and then its row (80), a cheque its row (20) and then its documents; a create locks its bank (10) first. A part is live while its source is (the row completed, not deleted, not a reversal; the cheque not deleted, bounced or returned): a voided receipt or a bounced cheque stops settling at once and takes no new parts (409 `SETTLEMENT_SOURCE_NOT_ALLOCATABLE`). A cheque without a status (an imported row) counts as live. Such a dead source also shows nothing on documents (`allocatedAmount` of the lists and its window). A part sits only on a final document or a sales proforma (`SETTLEABLE_DOCUMENT_STATUSES`): a draft is not listed and takes no new part (422 `SETTLEMENT_DOCUMENT_NOT_ISSUED`); a draft a source already settles (a proforma sent back to draft, or an old direct link) stays in that source's window and keeps its part or a smaller one, never a larger one. Reading or writing parts (`GET`/`PUT …/allocations`, and a create that sends `allocations`) needs the documents section as well (`READ_PERMISSIONS.documentRecord`, else 403 `SETTLEMENT_DOCUMENTS_NOT_READABLE`), and the forms hide «نشاندن روی فاکتورها» without it. A draft or proforma that a receipt, payment or cheque settles (a direct link or a part) keeps its party and currency when edited (409 `SETTLED_DOCUMENT_IDENTITY_LOCKED`), and its payable stays at least what settles it and its returns (409 `SETTLED_DOCUMENT_BELOW_SETTLED`, `src/services/documents/settledDocumentEdit.ts`), also when the VAT sent with its finalize would lower it; the edit holds the document `FOR UPDATE`, which a new settlement waits for. A document's paid amount is its direct-linked rows plus its live parts (`fetchLiveAllocations` in `src/services/documents/documentAllocations.ts`), its page lists the parts (treasury readers only, as the rows), a row with parts is not moved to another document (409 `TREASURY_ROW_HAS_ALLOCATIONS`), and an invoice with live parts is not voided (409 `DOCUMENT_HAS_TREASURY_ROWS`, naming each part). The voucher of the receipt or cheque never changes: the party's account takes the whole amount either way. Guards `reg_receipt_settles_invoices_v11_6_1t_m11`, `reg_direct_link_and_returns_v11_6_1t_m11`, `reg_cheque_settles_invoices_v11_6_1t_m11`, `reg_settlement_parts_race_v11_6_1t_m11`, `reg_settlement_review_guards_v11_6_1t_m11`, `reg_settlement_second_review_v11_6_1t_m11`.

- **Bank Statement Days (v11 phase 6.1ch, M13):** The bank reconciliation window reads the account's treasury rows when a statement is processed and only within the statement's first and last day widened by `BANK_STATEMENT_DATE_MARGIN_DAYS` (31) on each side (`statementCandidateRange` in `bankStatementMatcher.ts`, `bankReconcileCandidatesQuery`), so a match by tracking number up to a month away is still found; a statement with no date that can be read reads every row of the account, as before. A tracking-number match more than a month from the statement's days is no longer found (accepted for the smaller read). The account cannot be switched while a statement is processed.
- **History Import From Excel (v11, the 1405 replay, tool 1):** the owner decided that all of 1405 is entered again from 1 Farvardin, each document on its own day. Past receipts, payments, transfers between our accounts and wage payments come from the old Excel sheets «دریافت و پرداخت» and «هزینه‌ها» through «ورود سابقه از اکسل» on the treasury page (`POST /accounting/treasury/history-import`, permission `accounting.history_import`, given to no role by default; `src/services/historyImport/treasuryHistoryImport.service.ts`). The window (`src/components/historyImport/`) reads the sheets by their headers (`src/lib/historyImport/treasurySheet.ts`), takes one Jalali month at a time and maps each name once (bank or cash account, party, colleague, one of our accounts for a transfer, a counter account, or «کنار گذاشته شود»; the mapping is kept in the browser); a colleague's row is wages (paid through their approved payslips, oldest first, never beyond what they have left), an advance, or another payment with a counter account. Every row goes through the service a hand entry uses (`createTreasuryTransaction`, `createTreasuryTransfer`, `registerPayrollPayment`, each taking the import's transaction as a savepoint through `idempotentTransactionIn`), so its row, draft voucher, audit row and event are a hand entry's; a customer receipt and a supplier payment settle that party's open documents dated on or before the row (invoices only for a receipt, never a proforma) oldest first, read in the import's transaction; a payslip row needs `piecework.pay`, as the payslip payment form does (403 row error `HISTORY_IMPORT_PAYSLIP_FORBIDDEN`). Rows are posted by day (money in, transfers, money out, wages, then the Excel order); a row with no date, or dated before the last movement of one of its accounts (its live treasury rows, any live voucher line carrying its bank detail such as a cleared cheque or a void's reversal, a line without detail only when no other bank shares its ledger account, and its opening day; a bank opened in Aban cannot pay a Farvardin row) is refused, so `postTreasuryRow`'s balance check is the balance of the row's own day; a row like one in the file or in the books (same day, account, kind, amount and party) waits until the user accepts it; the Excel reference and cost centre go into the description. The preview runs everything in one transaction that is rolled back, with placeholder voucher and treasury numbers (`withDryRunNumbering`, `src/lib/dryRunNumbering.ts`), so it refuses exactly what posting would and leaves no row, audit row, event or sequence gap; the post keeps every row or none (`runImportRows`, `src/services/historyImport/importRunner.ts`) and is first tried as such a preview, so a refused post takes no voucher or treasury number. One import runs at a time (a transaction advisory lock) and locks its accounts before reading them, so a resend, another month or a hand entry cannot slip between the checks and the post; a post takes an Idempotency-Key, whose post pass completes it in its own commit, while a preview takes none; a post that recorded nothing answers 409 `HISTORY_IMPORT_NOT_POSTED` with every row's outcome, so its key is released and the fixed file is posted with it (the `onlyWhen` option of `idempotency()`). An amount is plain decimal text (no exponent, at most four decimals; `historyImportAmount`), and a request holds at most 250 rows (a month is about 150), so a post answers well within the proxy's timeout and holds its locks briefly. The service is a composition root of the package boundary, as the workflow domain actions are. Guards `reg_history_import_treasury_v11`, `reg_history_import_treasury_refusals_v11`, `reg_history_import_treasury_races_v11`, Vitest `treasuryHistorySheet.test.ts`.
