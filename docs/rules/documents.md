# Sales and Stock Documents

Document statuses and types, VAT, line discounts, service charges and exchange rates.

Core rules (one line each, with their guards) are in `AGENTS.md`. This file holds the full rules of the area; the history of each rule (versions, debt rows, decisions) is in `docs/rules/archive/AGENTS_V10.md` at commit `c3c9858` (archives removed in v11.1.0), and a «§N» below refers to that file's numbering.

- **Document Statuses:** Supported document statuses in API and services: `'draft'`, `'proforma'` (پیش‌فاکتور), and `'final'`. A document in `proforma` status, of type `proforma` or `invoice`, finalizes into an `invoice` dated the finalization moment (business now) that takes the invoice series' next number of that date's fiscal year (Kardex rows, the voucher and events carry both); the proforma number and Jalali date are kept in its notes (`proformaInvoiceTarget` in `src/services/documents/proformaInvoice.ts`). A draft or final document is never created in a closed fiscal year, and an edit never moves a draft into another fiscal year that is closed, nor turns a document in proforma status (an invoice or receipt the finalize day would not date) into a draft there (`FiscalPeriodService.assertOpen` in `createDocumentWithDetails` / `updateDocument`; v11 phase 3.1, M23: such a draft could be saved but never finalized); a proforma is exempt, and so is a procurement order edited inside its own year, since both take the finalize day. A sales lead is «won» only with a live proforma: voiding the document a lead points to (`crm_leads.proforma_id`: its proforma, or the invoice finalized from it) releases the lead in the void transaction, its row locked before the items, and moves a `won` lead back to `proposal` / `active` with a note (`releaseLeadOfVoidedDocument` in `src/services/crm/leadProforma.ts`); a refused void leaves the lead unchanged. Since v11 phase 6.4 (C3), finalizing that proforma into an invoice moves its lead to «won» in the finalize transaction, and the lead keeps «won» until the invoice is voided (`docs/rules/crm.md`). Only a sales document (`invoice` / `proforma` type) in `draft` or `proforma` status enters the approval workflow, inside its create transaction (`needsApprovalWorkflow` in `src/services/documents/documentApprovalScope.ts`, `maybeStartWorkflow({ tx })`; a start error rejects the document); the browser never starts it. A document in its approval workflow (any instance running, or its latest one rejected and waiting for its sender to correct and send it again, when the design has an action out of that step) is finalized only by its workflow from the approval inbox: `PUT /documents/:id/finalize` refuses it with 409 `DOCUMENT_IN_WORKFLOW` naming the current step from the instance's snapshot (`assertDocumentWorkflowClosed` in `src/services/documents/documentOpenWorkflow.ts`; v11 phase 6.1p, decision ت۵۲-۴; before, that route closed the running instance and skipped its steps). A document without one (none, the latest completed or closed by an admin, or rejected in a design with no way back) finalizes there as before, and a document already final stays an idempotent 200. The reject step of a document's workflow (`rejected`) turns a sales proforma back into a draft in the transition's transaction, so it stops reserving, with an audit row (`returnRejectedProformaToDraft` in `src/services/documents/documentWorkflowAction.ts`). A document whose workflow is running and past its initial step is not edited: `updateDocument` refuses it under the document row lock with 409 `DOCUMENT_IN_REVIEW` naming the step (`assertDocumentNotInReview` in `src/services/documents/documentReviewLock.ts`); a saved sales proforma above the sellable stock is kept and `POST` / `PUT /documents` answer one warning per short item in `stockWarnings` (`proformaStockWarnings` in `src/services/documents/documentSellableGate.ts`, the outflow gate's rule), which the invoice form shows. Voiding a document, deleting or voiding a draft journal voucher, and deleting an item, bank account or purchase requisition terminate the entity's running workflow in the same transaction, after the entity row lock: instance `TERMINATED`, pending tasks `canceled`, one `terminate` history row. A proforma with `crmLeadId` is created in one transaction with its lead: `lockLeadForNewProforma` locks the lead row before the document and refuses a second proforma under that lock, the document is created with `externalTx`, and `markLeadProforma` writes the link and the note in the same transaction (`src/services/crm/leadProforma.ts`). Linking or unlinking a document and a lead (`crmLeadId` on `POST` / `PUT /documents`) needs `crm.manage` (`CRM_LEAD_LINK_PERMISSION`, else 403 `CRM_LEAD_LINK_FORBIDDEN` before any write), and a proforma is never linked to a closed lead, won or lost by stage or status (`isClosedLead`, 422 `CRM_LEAD_CLOSED` under the lead lock), so a closed lead never goes back to `proposal`. `PUT /documents/:id` links or unlinks a lead the same way inside the edit transaction: `lockLeadsForDocumentLink` locks the leads linked to the document and the new one, in id order, before the document row; a missing lead is 422 `CRM_LEAD_NOT_FOUND` and, for a proforma, a lead with another proforma 422 `CRM_LEAD_HAS_PROFORMA`, both before any write; `applyDocumentLeadLink` writes `crm_lead_id`, marks the new lead and releases an unlinked one with a note. No route writes `documents.crm_lead_id` itself: `POST /documents` passes the checked id to the document insert. `PUT /documents/:id` requires the document's `version` (400 without it) and refuses a stale one with 409 `OCC_CONFLICT`; the invoice form sends the version of the proforma it loaded.

- **Structured VAT:** Sales VAT lives only in `documents.vat_percent` / `documents.vat_amount` (resolved via `src/services/documents/documentVat.ts` on create, update and finalize). The server computes the amount from `vat_percent`; an explicit amount sent with a positive percent must equal it, else 422 `VAT_AMOUNT_MISMATCH`, and the sales invoice form sends only the percent. The percentage amount is rounded to the document currency's smallest unit (`currencyScale` in `src/lib/currencyScale.ts`: rial without decimals, other currencies two decimals). A sales return carries VAT too: with an original invoice its percent is the invoice's and its amount the invoice VAT's share of the returned net, counted over the invoice's final returns — share(n) = min(invoice VAT, invoice VAT × n ÷ invoice net rounded like TD-382), this return's VAT = share(earlier + this) − share(earlier) — so returning the whole invoice in parts gives back exactly its VAT; another percent or amount is 422 `RETURN_VAT_MISMATCH` (`resolveReturnVatFromInvoice` in `src/services/documents/salesReturnVat.ts`, on create, draft edit and finalize). A return without an original invoice takes the user's percent. The return voucher debits sales returns with the net and VAT payable (3203) with the VAT and credits the customer with both. The sales voucher reads `vat_amount` only — NEVER parse amounts, rates or taxes out of free-text `notes`, and never write VAT into notes. Payable amount = net of lines + `vat_amount` (+ `service_charge_amount`, see below). The VAT base (Σ quantity × unit price − Σ line discount, each rounded to 4 places, never below zero) and the percent amount come from one place, `vatBaseOf` / `percentVatOf` in `src/lib/vatFormula.ts`, read by the server (`resolveDocumentVat`, `computeNetAmount`), the invoice form (`computeInvoiceTotals`) and the WooCommerce order percent; never write the formula again.

- **Line Discount Within the Line:** A document line's discount is at most its quantity × unit price; create and update refuse a larger one with 422 `LINE_DISCOUNT_EXCEEDS_AMOUNT` (`assertLineDiscountsWithinAmount` in `src/services/documents/lineDiscount.ts`), and the sales invoice form checks the same rule before adding a line (`lineDiscountError` in `src/lib/invoices/invoiceLine.ts`).

- **Service Charges:** A sales invoice's shipping and fee charges live only in `documents.service_charge_amount` (non-negative; set by the WooCommerce sync from `shipping_lines` / `fee_lines`, while the order's `total_tax` goes to `vat_amount`, with the percent `total_tax` ÷ net × 100 to two decimals when that percent rebuilds the tax exactly, else no percent (`wooOrderVatPercent` in `src/services/woocommerce/wooOrderVat.ts`)). The sales voucher credits them to «درآمد حمل و خدمات» (`serviceRevenueAccountCode`, default 5004). Payable amount = net of lines + `vat_amount` + `service_charge_amount`; a WooCommerce order whose invoice total differs from the order `total` is rejected as a whole.

- **Structured Exchange Rate:** The exchange rate of a non-IRR document (IRR per one unit of its currency) lives only in `documents.exchange_rate` and is required on create, update and finalize (`src/services/documents/documentExchangeRate.ts`). Vouchers take it via `VoucherSyncService.resolveVoucherExchangeRate` (explicit rate, document column, `exchange_rate_<cur>` setting) and never from `notes`; without a rate no voucher is issued (never rate 1). Pre-v7.0.63 documents were not migrated. Foreign-currency treasury receipts, payments and transfers follow the same rule (`resolveTreasuryExchangeRate` in `src/services/accounting/treasury/treasuryExchangeRate.ts`): explicit rate, else the rate of the invoice being settled (`documentId`, same currency), else the `exchange_rate_<cur>` setting; without a rate the transaction is refused, and its voucher rows carry the transaction's currency and rate.
