# Payroll and Piecework

Payslips, payments, eidi, statutory deductions and piecework. Insurance (3204), payroll tax (3206) and VAT are supported but never required, per person and per document; eidi is tax-free.

Core rules (one line each, with their guards) are in `AGENTS.md`. This file holds the full rules of the area; the history of each rule (versions, debt rows, decisions) is in `docs/rules/archive/AGENTS_V10.md`, and a «§N» below refers to that file's numbering.

- **Payroll Status:** Only `draft` and `approved` are set by hand (`updatePayrollStatus`); `paid` / `partially_paid` come only from `registerPayrollPayment` and voiding only from `deletePayroll`, and a payroll with any payment keeps its status; only an approved or partially paid payslip takes a payment (`isPayablePayrollStatus` in `src/lib/payroll/payrollPayable.ts`, shared with the payslip list and payslip; a draft is 409 `PAYROLL_NOT_APPROVED` and the screens offer «تأیید فیش» instead), and the payment date, method and reference are written only by the payment (the status route refuses them with 400). A payslip is issued as `draft` with its work logs; approving it, or putting an approved payslip without payment back to draft, needs `piecework.payroll_approve` (requires `piecework.view`; migration 0101 gave it to every role holding `piecework.payroll`, logged in `activity_logs`), and the payslip's issuer (`created_by_id`) never approves it, pays it or voids its payment (403 `PAYROLL_ISSUER_CANNOT_APPROVE` / `PAYROLL_ISSUER_CANNOT_PAY`, `assertPayrollDutyNotByIssuer` in `src/services/piecework/payrollDuties.ts`, rule `payrollIssuerDutyRefusal` in `src/lib/payroll/payrollDuties.ts` shared with the payslip screens, which disable the button and say why); the system admin is exempt. A draft payslip is edited only through `PUT /piecework/payrolls/:id` (`piecework.payroll`, strict body: title, bonuses, deductions with their description, advance deduction; `editDraftPayroll` in `src/services/piecework/payrollDraftEdit.ts`): under the personnel then payslip row locks, with the issue rules, its draft voucher voided through `voidSourceVoucher` and rebuilt in the same transaction and its audit row written with `tx`; an approved payslip is 409 `PAYROLL_NOT_DRAFT` and one whose voucher is no longer a draft 409 `PAYROLL_VOUCHER_NOT_DRAFT`. Insurance, payroll tax and VAT are supported but never required, per person and per document: a payslip suggests them only for personnel marked for them (off by default), an invoice carries VAT only when its form ticks it (off by default), and no report treats their absence as an error. Statutory deductions come from the Jalali year of the payslip's end date (`payroll_statutory_rates`, migration 0104, one row per year: employee and employer insurance percent, monthly insured wage cap, monthly tax brackets and whether the employee share leaves the tax base; written only with `payroll.rates_manage` from the «نرخ‌های بیمه و مالیات حقوق» settings tab, OCC by `version`, audited) for a personnel marked «مشمول بیمه» / «مشمول مالیات حقوق» (`personnel.is_insurance_covered` / `is_tax_covered`): `suggestStatutoryDeductions` (`src/lib/payroll/statutoryDeductions.ts`, shared with the form) takes the insurance base min(gross, cap × month fraction) and scales the brackets by the month fraction; issue and draft edit store `employee_insurance`, `employer_insurance` and `income_tax` (`resolvePayrollStatutory` in `src/services/piecework/payrollStatutory.ts`), an amount not sent is the suggestion and another one needs `statutoryOverrideReason` (422 `PAYROLL_STATUTORY_OVERRIDE_REASON_REQUIRED`), a year without rates issues with `statutoryWarning` and zero amounts; net = gross − employee insurance − income tax − other deductions − advance, the employer share is never taken from net; the voucher credits insurance payable (3204, `insurancePayableAccountCode`) with both shares, debits «هزینه بیمه سهم کارفرما» (7013, `employerInsuranceExpenseAccountCode`) and credits payroll tax payable (3206, `payrollTaxPayableAccountCode`), or the payslip is refused (`PAYROLL_STATUTORY_ACCOUNT_MISSING`); the form reads `GET /piecework/statutory-preview` and sends only the amounts the user typed (`statutoryPayloadOf` in `src/lib/payroll/statutoryForm.ts`). «فهرست بیمه و مالیات» (`GET /piecework/reports/statutory?year&month`, payroll readers; `payrollStatutoryReport` in `src/services/piecework/payrollStatutoryReport.ts`, contract and Excel rows in `src/lib/payroll/statutoryReport.ts`) sums per personnel in SQL the approved, partially paid and paid payslips whose period ends in that Jalali month, counts the drafts it leaves out and exports an xlsx file, listing only personnel whose month has insurance or payroll tax and counting the others (`withoutStatutory`); a month's insurance or payroll tax is paid to the organisation in one treasury payment from that list («پرداخت بیمه ماه» / «پرداخت مالیات حقوق ماه», `accounting.treasury`; `GET /accounting/treasury/statutory-remittance/options` (`statutoryRemittanceOptions.ts`) and `POST /accounting/treasury/statutory-remittance`, `createStatutoryRemittance` in `src/services/accounting/treasury/statutoryRemittance.ts`, rules shared with the window in `src/lib/treasury/statutoryRemittance.ts`): under the bank then the personnel row locks it writes one rial payment and one draft voucher with a debit row per person on the mapped 3204 or 3206 account with that personnel's detail, an optional late penalty on a chosen counter account other than 3204, 3206 or a treasury ledger account (422 `STATUTORY_REMITTANCE_PENALTY_ACCOUNT_INVALID`), and the lines in `statutory_remittance_lines` (migration 0118); read after those locks, each person's amount is at most what the month's list leaves after the live lines of that kind and month (else 422 `STATUTORY_REMITTANCE_EXCEEDS_MONTH`, so a paid month is never paid again while a later month keeps the balance up) and at most their credit balance on that account (else 422 `STATUTORY_REMITTANCE_EXCEEDS_BALANCE`); while the account's rows without a personnel detail (treasury form, cheque book, manual voucher) still carry a net debit, the list names those vouchers and the payment needs `acknowledgeUnassignedPayments` («بررسی کردم», else 409 `STATUTORY_REMITTANCE_UNASSIGNED_PAYMENTS`); the list offers only personnel with that month's insurance or tax, a voided payment's lines no longer count, it is voided like any treasury row, the treasury form and the cheque book warn when their counter account is 3204 / 3206 (`statutoryKind` of the counter-account list), and the financial health check lists the vouchers of such an account (`statutory_payments_without_personnel`) until a correction voucher moves them to each person; payslips issued before the split (`is_statutory_split = 0`) whose deductions still sit on 3205 are only listed by the financial health check (`payroll_deductions_before_statutory_split`) until a correction voucher moves them. Eidi is optional per person too: a personnel with «مشمول عیدی» off (`personnel.is_eidi_covered`, migration 0117, on by default, kept on an edit that does not send it) gets no eidi share, eidi year or rate warning on a regular payslip, no yearly entitlement in the eidi payslip suggestion (only the accrual recorded earlier) and no row of its own in «عیدی سال» (`isEidiCovered` in `src/lib/payroll/eidi.ts`). Eidi comes from the method of the Jalali year of the payslip's end date (`payroll_eidi_rates`, migration 0105, one row per year, written with `payroll.rates_manage` in the same settings tab, OCC by `version`, audited): «درصدی از کارکرد تا سقف سال» (`percent_of_work`), «قانون کار» (`labor_law`) or «مبلغ ثابت» (`fixed_amount`) (`src/lib/payroll/eidi.ts`, shared with the forms); with the percent method each regular payslip stores its share, round((piecework + fixed salary) × percent) up to what the year cap leaves after the personnel's other live shares, in `eidi_amount` / `eidi_year` (`resolvePayslipEidiShare` in `src/services/piecework/payrollEidi.ts`; a year without rates issues with `eidiWarning`), never taken from net, and its voucher debits «هزینه عیدی و پاداش» (7014, `eidiExpenseAccountCode`) and credits «عیدی پرداختنی» (3207, `eidiPayableAccountCode`); an «فیش عیدی» (`POST /piecework/eidi-payslips`, `piecework.payroll`, `is_eidi = 1`, `issueEidiPayslip` in `src/services/piecework/payrollEidiPayslip.ts`) takes the server's suggestion (the approved accrual not yet paid out, else the year's entitlement pro rata by days of service less earlier eidi payslips), another amount only with a reason (422 `PAYROLL_EIDI_OVERRIDE_REASON_REQUIRED`), debits 3207 with the accrued part and 7014 with the rest, carries no payroll tax and posts nothing to 3206, whatever the personnel's tax coverage (v10.0.234: the rates and the payslip take no tax field, migration 0106 dropped `tax_exempt_cap`), and follows the payslip's approval and payment rules; an eidi payslip is never edited as a draft (409 `PAYROLL_EIDI_NOT_EDITABLE`) and a payslip whose share an eidi payslip has paid is neither deleted nor put back to draft (409 `PAYROLL_EIDI_ALREADY_SETTLED`, under the personnel then payslip row locks); a payslip voucher is dated the earlier of its period end and the business today, so an eidi or prepayment payslip issued early is never dated in the future (`payrollVoucherDate`); «عیدی سال» (`GET /piecework/reports/eidi?year`, `payrollEidiReport`) lists accrued, paid and remaining per personnel with an xlsx export. A new payroll counts only work logs with no payroll or whose payroll is deleted, and links them in the same transaction. Editing or deleting a work log locks its row in a transaction and accepts only such a free, unpaid log (`lockEditableWorkLog` / `workLogFreeOfLivePayroll` in `src/services/piecework/workLogPayrollLink.ts`). An advance deduction larger than the employee's outstanding advance (`getPersonnelAdvanceBalance`, read only from the personnel's rows on the advance account, mapped 1301; no rows = 0, never treasury rows — v9.0.267) is refused. Fixed salary is granted for every Jalali month of the payroll period, a partial month pro rata by days (`computeFixedSalaryShares` in `src/lib/payroll/fixedSalaryProration.ts`, shared by the server and the payroll form): a month's share is round(salary × cumulative days of that month ÷ its days) minus earlier payrolls' shares of it, recorded per month in `piecework_payrolls.fixed_salary_months` (migration 0048); a payroll issued before v8.0.30 counts as the full month of its start date. A personnel whose employment status is «قطع همکاری» earns fixed salary only up to its end date (`serviceEndOf` / `fixedSalaryPeriodEnd` in `src/lib/payroll/payrollPeriod.ts`, shared with the payroll form; the same pro rata rule) and nothing after it; such a personnel without a valid end date gets no fixed-salary payslip (422 `PAYROLL_SERVICE_END_DATE_REQUIRED`), and a payslip whose period ends after the business today is refused (422 `PAYROLL_PERIOD_IN_FUTURE`, `payrollPeriodFutureError`), unless it is a prepayment payslip (`is_prepayment`, migration 0103) with a required reason (`prepayment_reason`, kept in its notes, audit row and print): it pays fixed salary only and leaves the period's work logs free, and a prepayment without a reason, of an ended period or of a personnel without fixed salary is 422 `PAYROLL_PREPAYMENT_REASON_REQUIRED` / `PAYROLL_PREPAYMENT_NOT_FUTURE` / `PAYROLL_PREPAYMENT_FIXED_SALARY_ONLY` (`payrollPrepaymentError` in `src/lib/payroll/payrollPeriod.ts`, shared with the payslip form). Fixed salary starts on the personnel's service start date (`personnel.start_date`, ISO, empty = the period's first day; form, detail, Excel import and export), the first month pro rata by days (`serviceStartOf` / `fixedSalaryPeriodStart`, same file) (payroll duties plan §3.4). A payroll payment is voided only through `PayrollPaymentVoidService.voidPayrollPayment` (`POST /piecework/payrolls/:id/payments/:transactionId/void`, reason required; the treasury void still refuses payroll payments): in one transaction (locks bank → payroll → treasury row) the bank balance comes back, the payment voucher goes through `voidSourceVoucher`, a reversal treasury row (`reversal_of_id`) is recorded and the payment marked `voided`, and the payroll's paid amount, status and paid work logs step back; a payroll whose payments are all voided can be deleted. A payroll payment and its void write their «پرداخت حقوق» audit row with the payment's `tx`, the request and the amount (`auditPayrollPaymentRegistered` / `auditPayrollPaymentVoided` in `src/services/piecework/pieceworkAudit.ts`), and the void writes the reversal row's `TreasuryTransactionApproved` event to the outbox in the same transaction. A payroll payment has no cheque method: `registerPayrollPayment` refuses `method: 'cheque'` and the payment form offers bank transfer, cash and POS only. A payroll payment's date goes through `resolveTreasuryWriteDate` before its transaction, like every treasury write: empty is the business today, a non-existent day is 422 and a date after the business today 422 `TREASURY_DATE_IN_FUTURE`, with nothing written; the payment form sends an ISO date from `JalaliDateInput` (default today) and shows the server's message. Salary is settled only through the payslip payment: a treasury payment to personnel with purpose «settlement» is refused with 422 `TREASURY_PAYROLL_SETTLEMENT_VIA_PAYSLIP` before anything is written (`isSalarySettlementPayment` in `src/lib/treasury/partyPurpose.ts`, shared with the treasury form, which does not offer that purpose for a payment); a personnel receipt with purpose «settlement» stays accepted. A payslip's other deductions (3205) are remitted by a personnel payment with purpose «deductions» («واریز کسورات حقوق», offered for payments only), which debits the mapped `employeeDeductionsPayableAccountCode` with the personnel detail (migration 0109 widened `chk_treasury_transactions_purpose` and `chk_cheques_purpose`). A treasury advance (a payment to personnel with purpose «advance») that a live payslip has deducted is not voided: `voidTreasuryTransaction` locks the personnel row after the bank and before the treasury row (the lock payslip issue takes) and refuses with 409 `TREASURY_ADVANCE_DEDUCTED` when the personnel's advance balance without this advance's voucher rows would fall below zero (`assertAdvanceVoidKeepsBalance` in `src/services/accounting/treasury/advanceVoidGuard.ts`); an advance not yet deducted, or one whose payslip was deleted, is voided. A treasury receipt or payment linked to a document is at most the document's remaining amount (payable less its settled amount, `documentEventAmounts` and `settledAmount`), on create and on relink: more is 422 `TREASURY_AMOUNT_EXCEEDS_DOCUMENT_REMAINING` and the excess is recorded on account; the document is locked `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` after the bank, so concurrent payments to one document are checked one after the other (`lockSettlementDocument` / `assertWithinDocumentRemaining` in `src/services/accounting/treasury/documentRemaining.ts`). A payslip's bonuses, deductions and advance deduction are non-negative (route schema 400, `generatePayroll` 422 `PAYROLL_NEGATIVE_COMPONENT`, the form's fields `min="0"`); the payslip voucher credits wages payable with exactly the net or is refused (`PAYROLL_NEGATIVE_COMPONENT` / `PAYROLL_VOUCHER_NET_MISMATCH`, never a silent null); a payslip is paid only with its live voucher (409 `PAYROLL_WITHOUT_VOUCHER`); invariant I10 (`src/tests/invariants/payrollInvariants.ts`) checks both, and older payslips that miss them are only listed by the financial health check (`payroll_voucher_mismatch`, `src/services/piecework/payrollVoucherHealth.ts`).

- **Piecework Rates and Work Logs:** A piecework task's base rate is a non-negative number (`parsePieceworkRate` in `src/lib/piecework/pieceworkRate.ts`, shared by the server and the Excel preview): the task routes refuse a text or negative rate with 400, the service with 422 `PIECEWORK_RATE_INVALID`, and the Excel import writes no row with one and lists it in its `errors` with the row number. A work log needs positive personnel, task and project ids, a quantity above zero or «hh:mm» (`parseWorkQuantity` in `src/lib/piecework/workQuantity.ts`) and a non-negative manual rate (route 400; `normalizeWorkLogEntries` in `src/services/piecework/workLogEntry.ts`, 422 `PIECEWORK_LOG_INVALID`), and its personnel, task and project must be live, read `FOR SHARE` (`assertWorkLogParentsLive`, 422 `PIECEWORK_LOG_PERSONNEL_INVALID` / `PIECEWORK_LOG_TASK_INVALID` / `PIECEWORK_LOG_PROJECT_INVALID`); `logWorkEntries` writes a batch in one transaction, so one bad row saves nothing, and editing a log follows the same quantity, rate and project rules. A log posted from the project workshop schedule (`ProjectScheduleTab`) carries its schedule row (`scheduleRef`: stage, product and row id; `scheduleLogItem` in `src/lib/projects/scheduleWorkLog.ts`) and no rate: the server always gives it the personnel custom rate, else the task base rate, and ignores a sent rate even from a rate manager. Such a log is written once per schedule row: `logWorkEntries` locks the project row `FOR UPDATE` before the parents' shared locks (every project of a batch with a schedule row, in id order, so a batch never deadlocks against its mirror), requires the row in the saved schedule with the same personnel and task (422 `PIECEWORK_SCHEDULE_ROW_NOT_FOUND` / `PIECEWORK_SCHEDULE_ROW_MISMATCH`), refuses a row that already has a live log (409 `PIECEWORK_SCHEDULE_ROW_LOGGED`) and writes the new log id into the row (`pieceworkLogId`, `src/services/piecework/scheduleRowLink.ts`); deleting the log or moving it to another project frees the row, a project save never sets or drops a link (`keepScheduleLogLinks`), and the tab saves the schedule before logging and reads the row state only from the server (`isScheduleRowLogged`). A schedule log above its row's quantity is 422 `PIECEWORK_SCHEDULE_ROW_QUANTITY_EXCEEDED`, and so is an edit that changes a linked log's quantity to above its row (`assertLinkedRowQuantity`, the project row `FOR UPDATE` after the log row; a stored log already above its row keeps its quantity and takes other edits), and no work log is dated, or moved, after a terminated personnel's service end, nor recorded for one without a valid end date (422 `PIECEWORK_LOG_AFTER_SERVICE_END`, `assertWorkLogsWithinService` in `src/services/piecework/workLogEntry.ts` with the payroll's `serviceEndOf`; both product-owner decision ت۱۰ of phase 5). The tab dates its logs with its own Jalali picker «تاریخ کارکرد برای ثبت کارمزد» (`JalaliDateInput`, default `getTodayIsoDate`), never the row or project start date. A personnel custom rate is saved only through `savePersonnelRate` (`src/services/piecework/personnelRate.ts`): in one transaction under the live personnel row `FOR UPDATE` and the live task `FOR SHARE` (else 422 `PIECEWORK_RATE_PERSONNEL_INVALID` / `PIECEWORK_RATE_TASK_INVALID`), a non-negative required rate, one active row per personnel and task (partial unique index `uq_piecework_personnel_rates_active`, migration 0076, created only on data without duplicates; the financial health check lists duplicates as `piecework_personnel_rate_uniqueness`), and each change writes a `piecework_task_rate_history` row with `personnel_id` (left out of the task base rate history) and a «نرخ اختصاصی پرسنل» audit row with before and after in the same transaction; a work log and the rates page both read the newest active row (`activePersonnelRate`). Every work log create, edit and delete and every payslip issue, status change and delete, and every task title create, delete, restore, clear-all and Excel import (entity «عناوین پرکیسی»), writes its own audit row inside its transaction (`src/services/piecework/pieceworkAudit.ts`): the routes pass `req`, the details carry the snapshot after a create, only the changed fields with before and after on an edit and the snapshot before a delete, and payslip and log statuses use the Persian labels of `src/lib/payroll/payrollStatusLabels.ts`, never the status code. The work log list is filtered in SQL (`GET /piecework/logs`, `workLogConditions` in `src/services/piecework/workLogList.ts`: personnel, project with `none` for no project, status with `processed` for any log on a payslip, a Jalali or ISO range and search; a malformed filter is 400); with `page` it returns one page `{ data, total, page, limit, totalAmount }` counted and summed in SQL, `GET /piecework/logs/summary` gives the page cards and the project labor costs, and the piecework page reads one page of the current Jalali month (`usePieceworkLogList`, contract `src/lib/piecework/workLogList.ts`) and the payslip preview from the server, never the whole ledger.

- **Payroll Actions by Their Own Keys:** Each payroll write asks the catalog key of its own name through `requirePermission` and the constants of `src/lib/permissions/pieceworkPermissions.ts`: task titles, categories, the Excel import, base and custom rates `piecework.manage_tasks`; work log create, edit and delete `piecework.log` (a manual rate on create or edit only with `piecework.manage_tasks`, otherwise a new log gets the server rate and an edited one keeps its rate); payroll issue, notes, voucher sync and delete `piecework.payroll`, its approval and return to draft `piecework.payroll_approve`; the yearly insurance and tax rates `payroll.rates_manage` (requires `piecework.view`, granted to no role by default); a payroll payment and its void the key `piecework.pay` (requires `piecework.view`; its holders also read payrolls and the bank pick list). `personnel.manage` covers the personnel dossier only and writes no piecework record; the task-titles settings tab opens for `piecework.manage_tasks`. Migration 0078 gave every role holding `personnel.manage` piecework.view / manage_tasks / log / payroll / pay and every role holding `settings.manage` piecework.view / manage_tasks, each change logged in `activity_logs`. The piecework page, the payslip and payment windows and the project workshop schedule show each button by the same key (`usePieceworkPermissions`).
