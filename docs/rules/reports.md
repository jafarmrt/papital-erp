# Financial and Management Reports

Trial balance, account and party cards, journal book, financial statements and management reports.

Core rules (one line each, with their guards) are in `AGENTS.md`. This file holds the full rules of the area; the history of each rule (versions, debt rows, decisions) is in `docs/rules/archive/AGENTS_V10.md` at commit `c3c9858` (archives removed in v11.1.0), and a «§N» below refers to that file's numbering.

- **Report Currency Rule:** A voucher row's currency is its own, else its voucher's, else IRR. Reports that sum voucher rows (trial balance, account card, party ledger, project reports, financial health check) use one rule from `src/services/accounting/voucherItemAmount.ts`: the «all currencies» view converts a foreign row to IRR at that row's own exchange rate (rounded to the rial) and keeps IRR rows as they are; a single-currency view shows only that currency's rows at their own amounts. Never add a raw foreign amount to rials.

- **Drill-Down:** All balance rows support instant navigation to detailed ledger cards (`ledger`). The card of a group or general account carries all its sub-accounts (`accountSubtreeCondition` in `src/services/accounting/accountSubtree.ts`), so it shows the balance the trial balance row shows. The card has one contract shared by the server and the browser (`src/lib/accounting/accountCard.ts`): the server sends the opening row (`isOpening`) and the view never adds its own.

- **Ledger Reports Need Accounting Keys:** `GET /accounting/reports/account-card`, `/ledger`, `/party-ledger` and `/parties` need `accounting.reports` or `accounting.view`; the customer page reads its own card from `GET /customers/:id/account-card`. An account card with no account, detailed type, id or name is 422 `ACCOUNT_CARD_FILTER_REQUIRED` and a party statement with no party 422 `PARTY_LEDGER_PARTY_REQUIRED` (`accountCardHasFilter` in `src/lib/accounting/accountCard.ts`, shared with the explorer, which sends no ledger request until a selection is made).

- **Party Statement Rows by Type and Id:** `getDetailedPartyLedger` counts for a customer or supplier the TD-416 rows (`partyDetailedRowsCondition`) and for a personnel the rows of type `personnel` with its id (`personnelDetailedRowsCondition` in `src/services/accounting/partyDetailedRows.ts`), plus legacy rows without an id under the exact current name; never an id of another detailed type or a «contains» name. The trial balance's detailed level keys a row by account, detailed type and id, and by name only for a row without an id. A party id is read from its own table (a soft-deleted record too); a missing one is 404 `PARTY_LEDGER_PARTY_NOT_FOUND`, and a name no party has matches only rows with exactly that trimmed name. A party's statement, its account card (`party`) and the customer and personnel delete guards count permanent accounts only (`permanentAccountCondition` in `src/services/accounting/partyTemporaryRows.ts`, `TEMPORARY_ACCOUNT_TYPES` in `src/lib/accounting/partyTemporaryRows.ts`); revenue, expense and cost-of-sales rows with the party's detail come apart in `temporaryRows`, shown by `PartyTemporaryRowsPanel` and outside the balance.

- **Journal Book Paged:** `GET /accounting/reports/journal-book` returns one page (`page`, `limit`; default `JOURNAL_BOOK_PAGE_SIZE` 200, at most 1,000) once, never `{ report, ...report }`; row numbers and the running balance come from window functions over the whole range, and the totals, voucher count and row count (`total`) are summed in SQL over the whole range (contract `src/lib/accounting/journalBook.ts`).

- **Accounting Buttons Ask Their API Key:** the accounting dashboard shows the voucher, treasury and cheque buttons only for `accounting.vouchers`, `accounting.treasury` and `accounting.cheques`, the year-closing execute button only for `accounting.fiscal_close` (others see the preview and a note), and the chart-of-accounts seed button only for the system admin (`useIsSystemAdmin` in `src/contexts/AuthContext.tsx`).

- **One Trial Balance Row per Detail Account:** the level-4 trial balance and its tree group a detail row by account, detailed type and id for customers, suppliers, personnel, projects and bank accounts and show the entity's current name (`trialBalanceDetailKey` / `currentDetailNames` in `src/services/accounting/trialBalanceDetail.ts`), whatever name each voucher stored; a legacy party row without an id keeps its exact name, a row without an id on a manual voucher keeps the typed name, and a row without an id of an automatic voucher is keyed by its stable reference, the voucher's cheque (`source_cheque_id`, shown «چک <number>») or the linked item of an item opening row (`item_opening_voucher_items`, shown with its current name and code), else it goes to the account's general row «سایر / عمومی».

- **Party Statement Print:** the party statement answers with its own `startDate` / `endDate` (null for an open end) next to its `currency`; the print header and the final balance read them from the response, a party, range or currency change reloads it, and «ماه جاری» / «سال جاری» start on the first day of today's Jalali month and year.

- **Report Digits, Currency Names and Today:** accounting reports show voucher numbers and counts in Persian digits and name currencies with `formatCurrencyLabel` (pickers list `TREASURY_CURRENCIES`), never a raw ISO code; the party statement's issue date and the party statement and account explorer export file names take today from the business time zone (`getTodayIsoDate` / `getTodayJalaliDate`, `partyStatementFileName` / `accountExplorerFileName` in `src/lib/accounting/accountingExport.ts`), never `new Date().toISOString()` or `toLocaleDateString` (Vitest `accountingReportDigits.test.tsx`).

- **Voucher List Paged on the Server:** The «اسناد حسابداری» tab reads one page from `GET /accounting/vouchers` with its page, search, `voucherType`, status and ISO dates (`useVoucherPageQuery`, contract `src/lib/accounting/voucherList.ts`); the response carries `total` and `statusCounts` (every status under the other filters), which the status tabs show. Never filter or count vouchers in the browser.

- **Statement Dates in Their Own Header:** The balance sheet and the financial ratios take an as-of date and the income statement its own period, each in its own header (`ReportDateFields.tsx`, ISO; empty = today / from the first entry); the income statement never reads the trial balance dates, and each statement shows its date or period in Jalali.

- **One Financial Statement Contract:** The income statement and the balance sheet have one contract shared by the server and the browser (`src/lib/accounting/financialStatements.ts`): `getIncomeStatement` / `getBalanceSheet` return it and `IncomeStatementView` / `BalanceSheetView` read only its keys (revenues, cost of sales, operating expenses; current and non-current assets, current liabilities, equity and the period profit); the net margin is shown in Persian digits, and none without revenue.

- **Party Account Card by Id:** A party's account card and balance (the customers page «تراز مالی», the customer dossier) come only from `GET /customers/:id/account-card` (`getCustomerAccountCard`): voucher rows of detailed type `customer` or `supplier` with the party's `detailed_id`, plus legacy rows without an id whose name equals the current name exactly (`partyDetailedRowsCondition` in `src/services/accounting/partyDetailedRows.ts`); never a «contains» name match. The generic account card's `detailedName` stays a search filter. The customer dossier's documents come from `GET /customers/:id/documents`: only sales documents (`invoice`, `proforma`, `return`) of the party, never a text search. A sales or purchase document (`invoice`, `proforma`, `return`, `receipt`, `purchase`) keeps its party in `documents.party_id` (FK to `customers`, migration 0080) and `buyer_name` is only its display name: `POST` / `PUT /documents` take `partyId` (the invoice form and the stock page send the picker's id, WooCommerce and procurement orders the matched customer or supplier), resolved by `resolveDocumentParty` in `src/services/documents/documentParty.ts` (a missing or deleted party 422 `DOCUMENT_PARTY_INVALID`, another type 422 `DOCUMENT_PARTY_NOT_ALLOWED`, a party of the wrong kind — a purchase document takes a supplier or «both», a sales document a customer or «both» (`partyFitsDocument` in `src/lib/documents/documentPartyKind.ts`, also filtering the stock page and invoice pickers) — 422 `DOCUMENT_PARTY_KIND_MISMATCH`, no id → the single live party of exactly that trimmed name, else none); a return with an original invoice takes the invoice's party (another one 422 `RETURN_PARTY_MISMATCH`). A document without a party id whose name is held only by a party of the other kind is refused with the same 422 `DOCUMENT_PARTY_KIND_MISMATCH` on create, edit and finalize, so a procurement order or a direct receipt never names a customer as supplier (`assertNameNotOtherKind`; the purchase forms list only suppliers and «both», `supplierNameOptions`). The sales, purchase and return vouchers (`documentVoucherPartyId`), the dossier and the party delete guard (`documentPartyCondition`) read the id, and a legacy document without one keeps the exact-name rule; migration 0080 linked old documents only to the single live party of exactly their name, and the financial health check lists the rest (`document_party_unlinked`), never rewritten. Sales lead values are summed only per currency: `GET /crm/stats` returns `pipelineByCurrency`, `wonByCurrency` and `stageCounts[stage].byCurrency` (`getCrmStats` in `src/services/crm/crmStats.ts`; an empty currency is IRR), the customer dossier uses `sumByCurrency` (`src/lib/crm/leadCurrencyTotals.ts`), and no total adds amounts of different currencies. Follow-ups come only from `GET /crm/followups`: no activity-date range, ordered by due date, paginated, with `dueCount` / `openCount`; the follow-ups tab, the dashboard widget, the tab badge and the stats counter use the conditions in `src/services/crm/crmFollowups.ts`, never the date-filtered activity list. The CRM customer filter sends `customerId`: `leadCustomerCondition` (`src/services/crm/crmLeadCustomerFilter.ts`) returns the party's leads plus legacy leads without an id whose trimmed customer or company name equals the party's current name. A sales lead is deleted only under its row lock (`deleteLead` in `src/services/crm/crmLeadDelete.ts`): a missing or deleted lead is 404, a lead with an active document linked by `crm_lead_id` or `proforma_id` is refused with 409, and activities of a deleted or missing lead are left out of the stats, the activity and follow-up lists and the due reminder (`liveLeadActivityCondition`). A CRM activity is saved only when its lead and party exist and are not deleted (`resolveActivityParents` in `src/services/crm/crmActivityParents.ts`, else 422); these columns have no database foreign key. A sales lead's `estimatedValue` and `probability` go through `decimalInput`: the amount is non-negative, the probability an integer from 0 to 100, and `stage`, `status` and `currency` come only from the lists in `src/lib/crm/leadFields.ts` (an empty currency or «ریال» is IRR). A follow-up is completed or reopened only through `PUT /crm/activities/:id/complete-followup` / `reopen-followup` (`setFollowupCompleted` in `src/services/crm/crmFollowupStatus.ts`): a target state under the activity row lock, a repeat changes nothing, and each change writes an audit row in the same transaction; never a two-way toggle. An activity is edited (`PUT /crm/activities/:id`, strict body with its `version`, 409 `OCC_CONFLICT` when stale; its lead and party never change) and soft-deleted (`DELETE /crm/activities/:id`) only with `crm.manage`, under its row lock and then its sales file's (`FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, so a removal of the file that commits meanwhile makes it 404) with an audit row written with `tx`, and an edit notifies only newly added mentions (`updateActivity` / `softDeleteActivity` in `src/services/crm/crmActivityEdit.ts`). A lead entering «lost» needs a reason from `CRM_LOST_REASONS` and a note for «other» (`lostReasonError` in `src/lib/crm/leadFields.ts`, shared with the form and the stage dialog; else 422 `CRM_LEAD_LOST_REASON_REQUIRED`, `resolveLeadLostReason`); a legacy lost lead stays editable without one. Every stage change of a lead (create, edit, proforma link and release, conversion) writes a `crm_lead_stage_history` row in its transaction (`recordLeadStageChange`, migration 0113), and `GET /crm/reports/funnel` (`crm.view`, contract `src/lib/crm/funnelReport.ts`) reads only that history: entered, advanced, conversion and average days per stage and the lost reasons, by business-day range and seller; changes before v10.1.18 were rebuilt once from each lead's activity rows (migration 0125, `erp_backfill_crm_lead_stage_history`, `crm_lead_stage_history.source` `backfill` / `backfill_estimated`) and the report counts the estimated entries (`estimatedEntries`). `GET /crm/followups` answers the user's own follow-ups (`ownFollowupCondition`: the linked personnel, else the legacy exact name) unless a `crm.manage` holder asks `scope=all` or a seller. The sales pipeline board has fixed-width columns (`PIPELINE_COLUMN_WIDTH_PX` in `src/lib/crm/pipelineBoard.ts`) in a horizontally scrolling row, never a grid narrower than its columns; a card is dragged to another stage through the same stage change as its picker (`stageAfterDrop`), so «won» still needs a live proforma and «lost» a reason, it shows the lead's nearest open follow-up (`nextFollowUpDate` of the lead list, `nextFollowupDatesByLead`), and a phone shows one stage per tab (browser test `e2e/crm-pipeline.spec.ts`). A party carries tags (`customer_tags` / `customer_tag_links`, migration 0115): a tag is created, renamed with its `version` (409 `OCC_CONFLICT`) and soft-deleted only with `customers.manage` (`/customers/tags` in `src/routes/customerTags.routes.ts`, `src/services/customers/customerTags.service.ts`), its live name unique by `lower(btrim(name))` (409 `CUSTOMER_TAG_NAME_TAKEN`, index `uq_customer_tags_name_active`), each change with its «برچسب مشتری» audit row written with `tx`; `POST` / `PUT /customers` save `tagIds` in the party's own transaction after its row lock (`setCustomerTags`, a missing or deleted tag 422 `CUSTOMER_TAG_INVALID`, a missing field keeps the tags) with the tags in the audit row, and `GET /customers` and `GET /crm/leads` filter by `tagId` (`customerHasTagCondition` / `leadCustomerHasTagCondition`). The sales page opens what `/crm?leadId=` and `/crm?activityId=` name (notifications, the dashboard) and then clears the address (`useCrmDeepLink`, rules in `src/lib/crm/crmDeepLink.ts`: an activity opens its sales file, else its party's dossier, else the activity book); a dossier opens only for a real party (`resolveDossierParty`: the file's party id, else the single party of exactly its name), never a made-up party, the customers page has the sales file window too and opens a file on the sales page, and «ویرایش» from a dossier reads the full record by id (`GET /customers/:id`, `customers.view`, 404 for a missing or deleted party) and clears the request from the address. The sales file table, the activity book and a dossier's activities page on the server (`GET /crm/leads?page=`, `GET /crm/activities?page=&limit=&partyId=&search=&type=`, `useCrmListPages`); the board reads the newest `CRM_BOARD_LEAD_CAP` (500) files and says so at the cap; the search waits for a pause, the pick lists load once, and a failed load shows an error with «تلاش دوباره».

- **Management Reports:** The «گزارش‌های مدیریتی» page (`/management-reports/:tab`; tabs in `src/lib/reports/managementReportTabs.ts`, each with its own `PAGE_ACCESS` row) holds the sales report, `GET /reports/sales` with `reports.sales` (contract `src/lib/reports/salesReport.ts`, service `src/services/reports/salesReport.service.ts`): final, non-voided sales documents (`invoice`, `proforma`) at their own date less final returns at the return's date, summed and paged in SQL, by item, customer, category, Jalali period, project or channel and a second dimension. The «all currencies» view converts each document at its own exchange rate and leaves out (and counts) a foreign document without one; a single-currency view shows only that currency's documents. Cost is the Kardex cost of the document's own rows (`out` for a sale, `in` for a return), and cost, gross profit and margin go only to item cost readers (`ITEM_COST_READ_PERMISSIONS`); quantity only with the item in a dimension or filter; VAT and service charge only without an item or category filter. The ledger line (`salesLedgerCheck`: the sales voucher's revenue and discount mapping and 5101, TD-260 amounts, drafts apart) goes only to `accounting.reports` / `accounting.view` holders and only without a party, item, category, project or channel filter. Jalali periods, range shortcuts and comparison ranges come from `src/lib/reports/reportPeriods.ts`, the Excel and print helpers from `reportExport.ts` / `reportWorkbook.ts` (file «گزارش-فروش-<period>.xlsx», right-to-left) and `ReportActions` / `ReportPrintHeader`, shared by the later tabs. The receivables and payables aging tab is `GET /reports/aging` with `accounting.reports` (`AGING_REPORT_PERMISSION`; contract `src/lib/reports/agingReport.ts`, SQL `src/services/reports/agingReportQuery.ts`): each party's balance as of the report date on the mapped trade receivables or payables account and its sub-accounts, from the live rows of live vouchers of any status except a closing run's vouchers (`source_fiscal_year`), the party found by the TD-416 rule and amounts by the TD-260 rule; credits settle the oldest debits first (FIFO in SQL) and what is left is aged by its voucher date into 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 91–180 and over 180 days, the other side shown apart as an advance; drafts count unless «فقط سندهای تأییدشده» and their share is shown apart; in a rial view the party's pending and (receivables only) bounced cheques and its last receipt or payment (a completed, unreversed treasury row or a cheque that did not bounce or come back) are separate columns. Each party links to its statement (`/accounting/reports?subTab=party_ledger&partyType=…&partyId=…`), which opens on that party (`initialPartyId`). The VAT tab is `GET /reports/vat` with `accounting.reports` (`VAT_REPORT_PERMISSION`; contract `src/lib/reports/vatReport.ts`, SQL `src/services/reports/vatReportQuery.ts`), this Jalali quarter by default: part 1 sums the final, non-voided sales invoices (and legacy final proformas) and sales returns of the range at their own date by VAT percent (a VAT without a percent is «نامشخص»), the net of lines by the document VAT rule, a foreign document at its own rate rounded to the rial per document and one without a rate left out and counted, return VAT reducing; it flags a VAT that is not its percent of the net, a VAT without a percent and a foreign document without a rate; an invoice without VAT is shown in the «بی مالیات» row and never flagged, since VAT is optional per document. Part 2 reads the mapped sales VAT account (`getSalesVatPayableAccount`, default 3203) and its sub-accounts like the aging tab (drafts counted and shown apart, closing runs left out): opening, credit and debit of the range by source (sales document, treasury and cheque, other; a reversal voucher takes its original's source and document, the `voucherSourceKinds` chain) and closing; the difference of the ledger's net movement with part 1 is listed per document or voucher, largest first (`vatDifferencesSql`). Purchase VAT is not recorded (ت۱۳) and the report says so; it is an aid to the accountant, never the return form. Part 4: the sales tab draws the net-after-returns trend by Jalali month (season or year past 24 months) and the category shares (eight and «سایر دسته‌ها») from `GET /reports/sales/charts` (`reports.sales`, the sales report's filters and counting rule; `salesChartSeries` in `src/services/reports/salesCharts.service.ts`, contract `src/lib/reports/reportCharts.ts`), and the aging tab its five buckets from its own totals; the charts are Chart.js, imported only by `ReportBarChart` through `React.lazy` in `ReportChartCard` (`src/components/reports/charts`), so the library is the separate `vendor-charts` chunk loaded only when a chart is drawn: one series, one colour, no legend, right to left, the report's table as its table view. The dashboard «فروش و مطالبات» widget (`SalesSummaryWidget`, plain SVG trend) reads `GET /reports/dashboard-summary` (`DASHBOARD_SALES_WIDGET_PERMISSIONS`: either key opens it, and each part comes only to its own key: month-to-date sales against the same days of last month with six months of trend for `reports.sales`, the aged receivables and their part over 90 days for `accounting.reports`). The purchases tab is `GET /reports/purchases` with the new key `reports.purchases` (`PURCHASE_REPORT_PERMISSION`, granted to no role; contract `src/lib/reports/purchaseReport.ts`, SQL `src/services/reports/purchaseReportQuery.ts`): final, non-voided receipts and purchases at their own date (no draft or purchase proforma), the supplier by `party_id` else the single live party of the exact name, by supplier, item, category or Jalali period and a second dimension, with quantity and average price when the item is in a dimension or filter, the currency rule of the sales report, comparison, the ten largest suppliers and items, Excel «گزارش-خرید-<period>.xlsx» and print.
